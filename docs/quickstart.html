    Quick start

    This takes about two minutes: share a terminal from your computer and open it on your phone.

    1. Share

    On the computer you want to share from:

    setu share --password

    setu asks you to choose a session password, then prints the invite:

    setu v1.0.0 - sharing /bin/bash peer-to-peer

CONTROL link - anyone holding it can type into this terminal. Keep it secret:
  browser:  https://dmdhrumilmistry.github.io/setu/#k=Pqv4WrBl...
  terminal: setu join 'k=Pqv4WrBl...'
  [QR code]

Security: password required, max 2 client(s). Compare the verification code shown on both sides.

This terminal is attached to the session. Type <Enter> ~ . to end sharing.
Press Enter to start...

    Press Enter. Your terminal is now attached to the shared session, so you see everything the other side does and can type along.

    To share one program instead of your shell, put it after --:

    setu share --password -- claude
setu share --password -- htop

    2. Join from your phone

    Scan the QR code with your phone's camera (or send yourself the browser link over a channel you trust) and open it.

    1. Enter the session password.
    2. Compare the verification code on your phone with the one the host terminal prints for the new client. They must be the same.
    3. Type. On a phone, use the key bar for Esc, Tab, Ctrl, arrows and ^C, and the pencil button to compose longer text.

    3. Join from another terminal (optional)

    setu join 'k=Pqv4WrBl...'

    Quote the link: it contains characters your shell would otherwise interpret. Type <Enter> ~ . to detach.

    4. End the share

    On the host, type <Enter> ~ . (Enter, then tilde, then period), or press Ctrl+C in a headless share. When the shared program exits, the share ends too.

    Treat the link like a password. Anyone with a control link (and the password, if you set one) gets a shell on your machine. Do not paste links into chats, issue trackers or AI prompts.
